Privacy Policy

Offline PDF Toolbox · Effective Date: October 22, 2026

Summary: Offline PDF Toolbox does not collect, transmit, store, or share your documents or personal information. All PDF processing happens strictly on your local PC.

1. Introduction

Offline PDF Toolbox ("the Application", "we", "us") is designed with a privacy-by-design, offline-first architecture. We believe that your documents belong strictly to you. This Privacy Policy explains how the Application operates and demonstrates our commitment to protecting your privacy.

2. Processing of Document Content

When you use Offline PDF Toolbox to merge, split, compress, rotate, rearrange pages, or convert images to PDF:

  • Local Execution: All parsing, rendering, and file operations are performed exclusively on your computer's local hardware (CPU/RAM).
  • No Network Transmission: The Application contains an architectural network guard that strictly prevents outbound HTTP/HTTPS requests. Your files never leave your device.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate any backend servers, remote databases, or cloud processing pipelines.

3. Zero Telemetry & Analytics

The Application does not collect analytics, telemetry data, device identifiers, or tracking metrics. We do not use third-party tracking SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry).

4. Local Storage and Preferences

The Application stores minimal preference data locally on your machine to improve your user experience:

  • User Preferences: Theme preference (Light/Dark/System), language selection (English/Vietnamese), and default output directory choice.
  • Recent Files List: File paths and page counts of recently processed documents so you can easily access them again.
  • Temporary Workfiles: Intermediate files created during heavy operations are kept in a private temporary directory and deleted immediately upon task completion or application exit.

You can clear your recent files history and reset all preferences at any time from within the Settings screen in the Application.

5. Microsoft Store Purchases and Licensing

If you purchase the optional Pro license, the transaction is handled directly and securely by Microsoft Corporation through the Microsoft Store commerce platform. We never receive, process, or store your credit card, billing address, or payment details. Microsoft's privacy practices are governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Offline PDF Toolbox does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates or Microsoft Store policy requirements. Any updates will be published with a revised "Effective Date" at this public URL.
