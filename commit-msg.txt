Audit va redesign man hinh Home: luong ro rang, the cong cu, danh sach gan day

Audit: logic xu ly PDF KHONG bi tron vao component UI. Toan bo xu ly PDF nam
o main/services (qpdf, pdf-lib, sharp) va worker PDF.js; component chi goi IPC
va hien thi. Vi vay CHON REFACTOR TAI CHO, khong rebuild lap UI. Thay doi duoc
lam trong khu khung hien co, giu nguyen mo hinh ba tang.

Redesign man hinh chinh:
- Luong: chon cong cu truoc, tha tep sau (ADR-016). Vung tha o Home hien bang
  chon cong cu ngay tai cho (khong phai hop thoai bat len) goi y dung theo so
  tep da tha.
- The cong cu: luoi 3x2, icon lon, ten, mo ta mot dong, phim tat Ctrl+N, bo ma
  F-01..F-06 khoi UI, trang thai hover/focus/pressed ro rang, la <button> that
  de dung bang ban phim.
- Danh sach tep gan day: hinh thu nho trang dau, so trang, dung luong, thoi
  gian tuong doi, loai trung theo duong dan, ba thao tac (Mo, Hien trong thu
  muc, Xoa), "Clear list" thanh menu phu co xac nhan, gioi han 5 dong kem
  "Xem tat ca", trang thai rong.
- Badge "Processed on your PC" nam canh tieu de, noi dung co max-width va
  can giua.
- Cai dat: them thu muc luu mac dinh va xoa lich su; tach thanh nhom Ngon ngu /
  Giao dien va Tep va quyen rieng tu.
- Them layer phu khi keo tep, phim tat Ctrl+O / Ctrl+1..6 / Ctrl+,.
- Design token tap trung (styles/tokens.ts) cho mau, spacing, radius, font size,
  shadow; buildTheme dung de su doi mau nhan va chu phu.
- Tieu de dung phan tu heading that (Fluent Title2/Title3 render ra <span>,
  khong doc duoc bang trinh doc man hinh).

Sua 3 loi do refactor gay ra:
- Ket qua doc tep ghi de dai trang nguoi dung vua nhap.
- Ket qua tac vu cu hien o cong cu khac.
- Muon nhip dung mot lan, thieu mau trong danh sach gan day.

Kiem thu: 137 don vi + 38 dau-cuoi + 7/7 luong thu cong (gop, tach, nen, xoay,
sap xep trang, anh sang PDF, cai dat).
